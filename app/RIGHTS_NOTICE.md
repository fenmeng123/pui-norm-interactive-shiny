# Questionnaire sources and release terms

## Questionnaire sources

**Chinese Y-IAT.** Hou, Y. (2013). Young wangluo chengyin liangbiao [Young’s Internet Addiction Test]. In C. Wan & W. Jiang (Eds.), *Zhonghua yixue tongji baike quanshu: Jiankang celiang fence [Encyclopedia of Chinese medical statistics: Health measurement volume]* (pp. 297–298). China Statistics Press.

**English IAT-R.** Mak, K. K., & Young, K. S. (2020). Development and differential item functioning of the Internet Addiction Test-Revised (IAT-R): An item response theory approach. *Cyberpsychology, Behavior, and Social Networking*, *23*(5), 312–328. [https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2019.0468](https://doi.org/10.1089/cyber.2019.0468)

The reference norms were developed using the Chinese Y-IAT. The English questionnaire
uses the corresponding 17 items from the IAT-R, omitting original items 4, 7 and 9.
The item wording differs between the two versions.
